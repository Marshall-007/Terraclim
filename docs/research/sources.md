# Sources — Vino viticulture & irrigation constants

Bibliography for `viticulture_findings.md` / `calibrated_constants.md`. Grouped by topic;
starred (★) entries are the strongest to name-drop to judges. Source tier noted:
**[peer-reviewed]**, **[extension]** (university/govt viticulture programs), **[FAO/standard]**,
**[industry]**, **[reference]** (encyclopaedic/secondary).

> Note: full-text retrieval was limited by the session's network policy; citations were
> located and cross-checked via web search (abstracts, extension summaries, encyclopaedic
> entries). Bibliographic details (authors, journal, year) are given so the team can pull
> full text to confirm before final publication.

## Core / cross-cutting (name-drop these)

- ★ **Matthews, M.A. & Anderson, M.M. (1988).** "Fruit ripening in *Vitis vinifera* L.:
  responses to seasonal water deficits." *American Journal of Enology and Viticulture*
  39(4):313–320. **[peer-reviewed]** — foundational: pre-veraison water deficit increases
  anthocyanins/quality; timing-of-deficit paradigm.
- ★ **Chapman, D.M., Roby, G., Ebeler, S.E., Guinard, J-X. & Matthews, M.A. (2005).** "Sensory
  attributes of Cabernet Sauvignon wines made from vines with different water status."
  *Australian Journal of Grape and Wine Research* 11:339–347. **[peer-reviewed]** — deficit →
  fruity/less vegetal; over-watered → vegetal/dilute. Direct sensory proof.
- ★ **Williams, L.E.** "Deficit irrigation of wine grape vineyards." University of California
  ANR. https://ucanr.edu/repository/a/?get=177652 (and publication list
  https://my.ucanr.edu/repository/a/?a=177656). **[extension]** — berry-size window, vigour,
  drip-vs-rain dilution nuance.
- **Allen, R.G., Pereira, L.S., Raes, D. & Smith, M. (1998).** *Crop Evapotranspiration —
  Guidelines for computing crop water requirements.* FAO Irrigation & Drainage Paper 56.
  Ch.6 Table 12 (Kc) & Table 22 (root depth, depletion p).
  https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e0b.htm · Ch.8 (stress) https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e0e.htm ·
  grape page https://www.fao.org/land-water/databases-and-software/crop-information/grape/en/
  **[FAO/standard]** — Kc 0.30/0.70/0.45, p=0.45, root depth 1–2 m.
- **Amerine, M.A. & Winkler, A.J. (1944).** Winkler Index / GDD base 10 °C, Regions I–V.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winkler_index · https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/27652
  **[reference]** — heat-summation basis; Stellenbosch = Region IV.
- **"Vineyard Irrigation: A Balancing Act with Consequences." (2026).** *AJEV* 77(1), 0770005.
  https://www.ajevonline.org/content/77/1/0770005 **[peer-reviewed]** — current umbrella review.

## 1. GDD model & phenology thresholds

- **Parker, A. et al. / "Predicting Key Phenological Stages for 17 Grapevine Cultivars
  (*Vitis vinifera* L.)."** *AJEV* 68(1):60. https://www.ajevonline.org/content/68/1/60
  **[peer-reviewed]** — stage base temps; budbreak 78–180 DD, bloom→veraison 556–800 DD.
- **Van Leeuwen, C. et al. (2022).** "Heat requirements for grapevine varieties is essential
  information to adapt plant material in a changing climate." IVES Open Science.
  https://ives-openscience.eu/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Heat_Requirements_Grapevine_Van_Leeuwen.pdf
  **[peer-reviewed]** — variety heat-demand thesis.
- **eVineyard (2017).** "Why the need to calculate growing degree days in vineyard?"
  https://www.evineyardapp.com/blog/2017/03/01/why-the-need-to-calculate-growing-degree-days-in-vineyard/
  **[industry]** — Chardonnay ~75 GDD budburst, ~345 GDD flowering (base 10).
- **WineWiki — Phenological Stages.** https://www.winewithseth.com/winewiki/phenological-stages/
  **[reference]** — Cabernet/Napa stage GDD (budbreak 50–100, flowering 350–400, veraison
  1100–1300).
- **Oregon Viticulture — Growing Degree Days.** https://oregonviticulture.net/gdd/gdd.html **[extension]**
- **WSU Viticulture & Enology — Growing Degree Days.**
  https://wine.wsu.edu/extension/weather/growing-degree-days/ **[extension]** — 30 °C cap practice.
- **New Mexico State University — Grapevine Phenology (H338).**
  https://pubs.nmsu.edu/_h/H338/index.html **[extension]**
